\section*{FAST CONCEPT CHECKS}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
TERM & MEANS / READS AS\\ \midrule
ATTRIBUTE / FEATURE & one column of $X$; measured variable used as input.\\
OBSERVATION & one row of $X$; one fish/robot/sample/person.\\
TARGET / LABEL & value to predict; numeric for regression, class for classification.\\
WEIGHT $w_j$ & effect of feature $j$ in a linear score; sign gives direction.\\
INTERCEPT / BIAS & constant added when all inputs are zero; use leading 1 in input vector.\\
PRIOR & class probability before seeing attributes.\\
LIKELIHOOD & density/probability of observed attributes inside a class.\\
POSTERIOR & class probability after seeing attributes.\\
LOADING & PCA weight of an original attribute in one PC.\\
SCORE & coordinate of an observation on a PC.\\
CENTROID & mean vector/profile of a k-means cluster.\\
RESPONSIBILITY & GMM posterior probability that component $k$ generated row $i$.\\
THRESHOLD & cutoff turning score/probability into a class.\\
REGULARIZATION & penalty on weights; shrinks weights to reduce variance.\\
OVERFITTING & low training error but poor unseen/test error; model too variable.\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\subsection*{MODEL CHOICE / STATEMENT CUES}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
CUE & CORRECT READING\\ \midrule
LOW BIAS, HIGH VARIANCE & model too flexible; increase $\lambda$, simplify features, or use more data.\\
HIGH BIAS, LOW VARIANCE & model too simple; add features, reduce $\lambda$, or use more flexible model.\\
TRAIN ERROR VERY LOW & not enough alone to prove overfitting; compare with validation/test error.\\
CV FOR COMPLEXITY & use validation/inner folds to choose; use held-out/outer folds to estimate performance.\\
LINEAR ANN ACTIVATIONS & many linear layers collapse to one linear model.\\
OUTPUT ACTIVATION & linear = real regression; sigmoid = binary probability; softmax = multiclass probabilities.\\
