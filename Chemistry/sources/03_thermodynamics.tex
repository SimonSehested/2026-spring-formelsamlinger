\section{Thermodynamics}

\subsection{First Law and Enthalpy}

\subsubsection{First law of thermodynamics}
\[
\Delta U=q+w
\]
Internal energy changes by heat \(q\) and work \(w\). Heat absorbed by the system is positive; heat released is negative.

\subsubsection{Pressure-volume work}
\[
w=-p_{\mathrm{ext}}\Delta V
\]
Expansion work is negative for the system when \(\Delta V>0\). The lecture sign convention connects constant-pressure heat to enthalpy.

\subsubsection{Enthalpy}
\[
H=U+pV, \qquad q_p=\Delta H
\]
At constant pressure, the heat exchanged equals the enthalpy change. Enthalpy is a state function.

\subsubsection{Reaction enthalpy}
\[
\Delta H_{\mathrm{rxn}}=H_{\mathrm{products}}-H_{\mathrm{reactants}}
\]
\(\Delta H_{\mathrm{rxn}}<0\) is exothermic and \(\Delta H_{\mathrm{rxn}}>0\) is endothermic.

\subsubsection{Standard enthalpy of formation}
\[
\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}
=\sum_i \nu_i\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm f}(\mathrm{products})
-\sum_i \nu_i\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm f}(\mathrm{reactants})
\]
Elements in their most stable standard state have \(\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm f}=0\). Coefficients from the balanced reaction multiply formation enthalpies.

\subsubsection{Hess's law}
\[
\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{overall}}=\sum_k \Delta H^\circ_k
\]
Enthalpy changes add because enthalpy is a state function. Reversing a reaction changes the sign of \(\Delta H\).

\subsection{Entropy and Gibbs Free Energy}

\subsubsection{Gibbs free energy}
\[
\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S
\]
Gibbs free energy combines enthalpy and entropy at temperature \(T\). Use \(T\) in kelvin and consistent energy units.

\subsubsection{Spontaneity criterion}
\[
\Delta G<0:\ \text{spontaneous}, \qquad
\Delta G=0:\ \text{equilibrium}, \qquad
\Delta G>0:\ \text{nonspontaneous}
\]
The sign of \(\Delta G\) predicts thermodynamic direction under the specified conditions.

\subsubsection{Temperature dependence of spontaneity}
\[
\Delta G(T)=\Delta H-T\Delta S
\]
If \(\Delta H\) and \(\Delta S\) are treated as constant, increasing \(T\) changes \(\Delta G\) through the entropy term.

\subsubsection{Standard reaction entropy}
\[
\Delta S^\circ_{\mathrm{rxn}}
=\sum_i \nu_i S^\circ(\mathrm{products})
-\sum_i \nu_i S^\circ(\mathrm{reactants})
\]
Entropy changes are calculated from standard molar entropies using the balanced reaction.

\subsubsection{Thermochemical equation scaling}
\[
\ce{A -> B}\quad \Delta H
\qquad \Rightarrow \qquad
\ce{2A -> 2B}\quad 2\Delta H
\]
Thermodynamic quantities for a reaction scale with the written chemical equation.
