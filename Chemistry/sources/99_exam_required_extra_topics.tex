\section{Exam-Required Extra Topics}

\subsection{Recurring Exam Methods}

\subsubsection{Ideal gas stoichiometry}
\[
n_{\mathrm{gas}}=\frac{pV}{RT}
\]
% Added from exam coverage
Gas volumes in exam stoichiometry are converted to moles before applying balanced reaction coefficients.

\subsubsection{Combustion enthalpy from formation enthalpies}
\[
\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm{comb}}
=\sum \nu\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm f}(\ce{CO2},\ce{H2O},\ldots)
-\sum \nu\Delta H^\circ_{\mathrm f}(\text{fuel},\ce{O2})
\]
% Added from exam coverage
The standard formation enthalpy of \(\ce{O2(g)}\) is zero. Combustion questions require a balanced combustion reaction first.

\subsubsection{Percent change in hydronium concentration from pH}
\[
\frac{[\ce{H3O+}]_2}{[\ce{H3O+}]_1}=10^{\mathrm{pH}_1-\mathrm{pH}_2}, \qquad
\%\text{ increase}=(\text{ratio}-1)100\%
\]
% Added from exam coverage
This method follows directly from the logarithmic pH definition and appears in ocean-acidification style questions.
